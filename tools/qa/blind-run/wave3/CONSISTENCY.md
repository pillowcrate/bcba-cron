# Pass 2 — deck-wide sweep

## What the sweep says about the deck

The Wave 3 days were not the problem. Of the confirmed contradictions, **three** involved a new
day and **the rest were pre-existing**, several of them in the original 63. The most serious
errors were in the oldest material, which has never been through a blind pass or a consistency

## HIGH severity — fixed

**Day 22 contradicted its own task code.** `taskDesc` is the verbatim 6th-edition A.1:
*"Identify the goals of behavior analysis as a science (i.e., description, prediction, control)."*
The body taught **four** goals including explanation, and `d22q1` keyed the four-goal option. A
learner would have been trained to give an answer the outline marks wrong. Body now teaches three,
with explanation as what demonstrated control yields; `d22q1` re-keyed (letter unchanged).

**Day 22 taught methodological behaviorism while calling itself radical.** `d22q2` keyed
*"Mental constructs are not observable or directly manipulable"* as the reason behavior analysis
rejects mentalism. That is precisely the position `d94q1` keys as **methodological** behaviorism —
the wrong answer — and day 94 is correct: radical behaviorism includes private events in its
subject matter. Day 22 was training the error its neighbour punishes. The key is now the
explanatory-fiction ground; the old key survives as a distractor labelled in the rationale.

**Day 11 called escape from a task positive reinforcement.** Verbatim: *"A client receiving escape
from a task after engaging in problem behavior has received positive reinforcement if escape is the
added stimulus."* The next paragraph of the same day says escape is negative reinforcement, and
`d11q2` keys negative reinforcement with *"Positive reinforcement by task removal"* as a scored-wrong
distractor. This is the single highest-frequency discrimination in the deck. Replaced with a genuine
example of the positive-is-not-pleasant point (a reprimand that increases calling out).

**`d79q3` contradicted day 101 and itself.** It keyed *"An increase in problem behavior following
reinforcement schedule thinning"* as **not** resurgence, while `d101q2` keys resurgence for exactly a
thinning scenario — and `d79q3`'s own rationale reads *"clinically significant in FCT schedule
thinning contexts."* Distractor replaced.

**`d104q3` misstated Code 1.05 and contradicted two other days.** It keyed practising outside
competence as a violation *"regardless of the availability of alternatives"*, rejecting the
supervision route. Code 1.05 verbatim: behavior analysts enter new areas *"only after accessing and
documenting appropriate study, training, supervised experience, consultation, and/or co-treatment
from professionals competent in the new area."* Day 112 keys "accept only with supervision" as
**correct**, and day 57 says practising outside competence *"without appropriate supervision"* is the
violation. Day 104 was the outlier and its own rationale conceded the point. Rewritten. Found
independently by three agents.

**Day 97 misused "systematic replication".** It called within-participant ABAB replication systematic
replication — the term day 67, `d28q2` and `d97q2` reserve for establishing *generality* under
deliberately altered conditions. Corrected to "replication of the effect within the participant".

## `d68q3` — took three attempts, and is the cautionary tale

Originally: *"the most effective way to reduce problem behavior competing with an appropriate
alternative"*, keyed to increasing reinforcement density, with extinction as a scored-wrong
distractor. That conflicted with day 82, which said omitting extinction leaves **no** functional
advantage.

First repair softened both sides. A blind re-check then found the item **still had two defensible
answers** — and the agent was right: under matching-law logic, driving reinforcement for problem
behavior to zero shifts the ratio at least as decisively as enriching the alternative. Removing
"most effective" had sharpened the ambiguity rather than removing it.

Second repair abandoned the intervention framing entirely. The item now asks what allocation
*tracks* — relative reinforcement — which is the actual teaching point, has one answer, and lets the
rationale say plainly that **both** manipulations shift allocation. That also resolves the day 82
conflict at the root.

**Lesson: when two options are both true, do not re-word them — change what the question asks.**

## The day 76 / day 109 contradiction — fixed in pass 2

`d76q1` keyed *"comparable steady rates in the alone condition and every other condition"* to
**automatic reinforcement**, while `d109q2` keys that same undifferentiated pattern to **modify and
repeat the analysis**. Both days predate Wave 3.

Fixed by changing `d76q1`'s **stem** to persistence *across repeated alone sessions* — which is
genuinely diagnostic, preserves day 76's teaching point (no mediator present ⇒ not socially
maintained), and removes the overlap with the undifferentiated-outcome pattern. Day 109 untouched.
Day 76's body sentence was changed to match. This is also the mechanism day 128 now names, so the
three days are consistent for the first time.
